Tap to Pay on iPhone — enablement, education & checkout walkthrough

High-fidelity wireframes of the Tap to Pay on iPhone experience in Self-sown, submitted per the Tap to Pay on iPhone App & Marketing Requirements and Review Guide (v1.6), "App Review" section. Screens reproduce the shipping UI pixel-layout and use verbatim in-app copy; screens rendered natively by iOS / the Stripe Terminal SDK (Terms & Conditions, the contactless tap UI, PIN entry) are outlined in orange and annotated.

PSP: Stripe (approved PSP, US). Deploy region: United States. A physical-device screen recording following the shot list at the end of this document can be supplied on request; per Apple guidance the native checkout tap UI is recorded with a second device.

Video 1 — New-merchant onboarding & the Tap to Pay awareness moment

A signed-in seller on an eligible iPhone (Tap to Pay-capable device, Stripe card charges enabled on their connected account) is shown a full-screen awareness moment the first time they open the app after the feature ships. The moment is shown once per seller account, so a shared device still reaches every account. From here the merchant can enable Tap to Pay immediately, which presents Apple's Terms & Conditions and then the merchant education screens.

PIN entry and accessibility

Some cards ask the customer to enter their PIN. When that happens, a secure PIN screen appears on your iPhone and the customer enters the PIN themselves — hand them the phone or tilt it toward them. The PIN screen supports iOS accessibility features such as VoiceOver, so customers who use them can enter their PIN independently.

If a card can't be read

Some cards are not able to complete contactless transactions using a PIN. If this occurs, simply ask the customer if they have an alternative contactless card or digital wallet and continue the transaction using Tap to Pay on iPhone. You can also switch the sale to Lightning, a Square payment link, or cash from the same checkout screen.
