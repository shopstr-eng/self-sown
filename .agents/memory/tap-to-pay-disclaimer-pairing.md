The `/tap-to-pay` product page exists to satisfy Apple's rule that the short disclaimer ("Terms apply.") in tight placements must click through to a page showing the full disclaimer. Its Legal section is the load-bearing content — never remove or soften it.

**Why:** Apple's Sept 2026 Tap to Pay on iPhone marketing rules require legal disclaimers on ALL advertising; the short form is only permitted with that click-through. In-app modals and future push/SEO placements all depend on the page staying live and verbatim.

**How to apply:** disclaimer/copy text lives in one shared module that web and mobile both import — never retype the strings. Every screen that pitches or teaches Tap to Pay renders the shared disclaimer footer (short form + click-through; Apple Pay service notice when the copy names Apple Pay). Modal layouts must stay scrollable so the notice and buttons remain reachable on small phones. New push/SEO copy blocks must end with the short disclaimer.
