---
name: Brand positioning is local food + artisan goods, not raw milk
description: marketing/SEO copy must not lead with or center raw milk — the platform positions itself around local food and artisan goods broadly
---

The platform's public positioning is **local food and artisan goods**, full stop. Raw milk is explicitly dismissed as a positioning category in `seo_strategy.md`.

**Why:** the owner directed a de-emphasis of the raw-milk focus in September 2026; raw milk was the founding wedge but is no longer the brand story.

**How to apply:**

- When writing or editing marketing copy, meta descriptions, JSON-LD, llms.txt/llms-full.txt, FAQs, or placeholder/preview content, lead with local food / farm-fresh / artisan / handmade categories. Do not introduce raw-milk-first lists or raw-milk-centered narratives.
- Category lists that happen to include dairy are fine; ordering raw milk first or framing the platform around it is not.
- Test fixtures that use raw-milk product names as sample data are acceptable.
